FAQ

Questions to answer before the first visit.

Straight answers about the work. These are not reviews, prices, or credentials.

What happens on a weekly cleaning visit?

The visit covers the surface and the waterline: skimming, brushing, emptying the skimmer and pump baskets, and vacuuming when dirt has settled. The technician also looks over the equipment pad.

How do you balance the water?

We test sanitizer, pH, and total alkalinity and adjust them so the water stays comfortable for swimming. Name your usual sanitizer here if you want homeowners to see it before they write.

Can you repair pumps, filters, and heaters?

Pumps, filters, heaters, automatic cleaners, and salt systems can be checked on site. You receive a quote for the repair before parts are replaced.

When should we book an opening or a closing?

Openings and closings follow the local swimming season. An opening usually means the cover comes off, the equipment is restarted, and the water is treated. A closing protects the lines and equipment and secures the cover. Put your booking months in this answer.

Which neighborhoods do you serve?

The towns in the service area are placeholders in brackets. Replace them with the places you actually drive to, and say so here if you only travel a set distance.

What do you need from us before a visit?

A way into the yard, access to the equipment pad, and a note about pets, locked gates, or a cover. If a heater, salt system, or cleaner is not working, say so in the quote request.
